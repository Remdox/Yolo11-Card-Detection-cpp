\section{Contributions}
This section details the individual contributions of the team members and the number of hours employed for the project's completion.
\\ \newline
With respect to the pipeline described in \ref{sec:experiments}:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Marco Annunziata handled the training of the model and exporting to ONNX format, the pre-processing,
    the model inference, the post-processing, the selection of the dataset and the manual labeling of the video ($\sim$44 hours);
    \item Hermann Serain took care of the terminal-based user interface and the metrics calculation system ($\sim$30 hours);
    \item Sveva Turola dealt with the management of video/camera streams and frame extraction, the implementation
    of card grouping, the development of the Hi-Lo counting system, the visualization of the running count, the manual labeling
    of the images and the report writing ($\sim$40 hours).
\end{itemize}
Each group member has tested multiple time the system ($\sim$4 hours/person).
